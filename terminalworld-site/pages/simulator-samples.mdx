# Simulator samples — what does each model think the terminal said?

The [world-model probe](/world-model-probe) measures env-token *perplexity*. This page makes those numbers tangible: instead of scoring the real observation, we **sample** it. For 10 held-out trajectories from unseen repos, each model is given the first 14 (command, observation) pairs **plus the 15th command**, and asked to produce the **15th terminal observation** — drawn **5×** at the exact SWE-bench eval settings (temperature 1.0, `max_tokens=4096`, `max_model_len=32768`). Generation is in agent framing (the observation is the next `user` turn), so these samples line up one-to-one with the PPL table.

Six checkpoints are sampled on the **same** 10 trials: `base`, and the three loss masks — (a) assistant-only, (b) full-transcript, (c) user-only — at 10K and (for a/b) 1M. Pick any 1–2 with the pills below.

## The headline is *scale*, not just the mask

Aggregating character-overlap to the real observation across all 10 trials (mean over 5 draws, "faithful" = ≥ 0.8 overlap):

| checkpoint | mean similarity | faithful trials | env-PPL |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| base | 0.04 | 0/10 | 2.27 |
| (a) assistant-only · 10K | 0.48 | 4/10 | 1.81 |
| (b) full-transcript · 10K | 0.45 | 3/10 | 1.16 |
| (c) user-only · 10K | 0.52 | 4/10 | … |
| **(a) assistant-only · 1M** | **0.07** | **0/10** | 3.33 |
| **(b) full-transcript · 1M** | **0.79** | **7/10** | 1.07 |

**1. At 1M, the masks split violently — and only this is visible in free sampling.** (a) **collapses to 0.07**: asked for the terminal's reply it continues *as the agent*, narrating a plan and issuing the next command, or emitting a degenerate fragment (` ``` `, `Observ`). It no longer knows the next turn belongs to the environment — catastrophic forgetting, made visible. (b) **sharpens to 0.79**: faithful file contents, often byte-identical (the fastify trial matches all 107 chars), and — the sharpest tell — it predicts the **empty** observation when a command prints nothing, where base hallucinates a 22,000-char directory listing.

**2. At 10K, sampling can't separate the masks — but the probe can.** All three 10K masks sample comparably (~0.45–0.52), yet the teacher-forced [env-PPL probe](/world-model-probe) *does* split them cleanly at 10K (b-10k **1.16** ≪ a-10k **1.81**). Temperature-1.0 sampling is simply too noisy to surface a gap this size; perplexity, which integrates over the whole distribution, is the more sensitive instrument. **The probe is the rigorous comparison; this page is the intuition for what the extremes feel like.**

**3. base has the format but not the control.** It knows observations open with `Observation:` and can echo a known license header, but it can't stop or stay grounded — it loops one token (`ibraries`, `expensive`) to the cap, or invents filesystem state. Its world-model competence is real but unreliable (median PPL 2.27, with a heavy tail — 17/100 held-out trajectories at PPL > 10).

**(c) is the new point.** At 10K, user-only (c) is the strongest 10K arm on samples (0.52) and on the `tbls` trial reaches 0.87 — matching (b) at *1M*. Whether that lead holds rigorously waits on (c)'s env-PPL (running) and its 100K/1M checkpoints (training).
