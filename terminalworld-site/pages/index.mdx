# TerminalWorld

**A world model for terminal agent training.**

TerminalWorld is an experimental research thread under the [Marin](https://github.com/marin-community/marin)
project. The motivating question:

> Can we replace the (slow, expensive) Docker-per-rollout regime that powers
> today's terminal-agent training with a *learned* simulator — a model
> trained to emit the bytes a real shell would emit?

Tracking issue: [marin-community/marin#5866](https://github.com/marin-community/marin/issues/5866).

## Plan, in one paragraph

We train a small terminal simulator on the user turns of the
[Nemotron-Terminal Corpus](https://huggingface.co/datasets/nvidia/Nemotron-Terminal-Corpus),
then SFT Qwen3-8B three ways — (1) on the raw corpus, (2) on the same corpus
with user-turn masking to reproduce
[Dimitris Papailiopoulos's ECHO](https://x.com/DimitrisPapail/status/2056368948870811746)
finding, and (3) on rollouts against the *learned* terminal. The three are
compared on [Terminal-Bench](https://www.tbench.ai/).
